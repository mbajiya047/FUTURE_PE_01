The Real Story

"I spent eight years writing software specs before I realized I was starving for real food."

Like a lot of folks in Bangalore, my twenties were a blur of 14-hour days, cold takeout eaten over a laptop keyboard, and bitter coffee swallowed solely for the caffeine hit. I bought bread that stayed suspiciously soft for three weeks on my kitchen counter and wondered why my stomach hurt every single afternoon.

In 2019, I spent three months on an organic farm in Pondicherry. An old French baker named Jean-Marc handed me a jar of bubbling wild starter, stone-ground flour, and well water. He told me: 'Arjun, real bread isn't manufactured. It's raised.'

“You cannot hurry a 36-hour wild fermentation with an agile sprint. The dough moves when it wants to move. It taught me patience I never had in tech.”

In 2021, I found this sunlit corner on 12th Main Road, Indiranagar. I brought in a stone deck oven, partnered directly with a multi-generation coffee family in Chikmagalur, and opened the doors. We aren't a venture-backed franchise and we don't plan to be. We just want to be your neighborhood bakehouse where you know exactly what went into your breakfast.

Arjun Sen
Founder & Baker · Roots & Whisk

Straight Talk

Questions Folks in Indiranagar Ask Us

No corporate PR responses. Here is how things actually work here.

Remote Work "Can I bring my laptop and work here?"

Tuesday through Friday: Absolutely yes. We have individual power outlets along the wall tables, reliable 250 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi, and our team will never hover over your table asking you to order more. On Saturdays and Sundays: We ask guests to keep the main dining tables laptop-free between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM so brunch diners and families don't face hour-long sidewalk queues.

Parking "Where on 12th Main can I park?"

If you ride a scooter or bike, park right in front of our verandah wooden gate. If you're driving a four-wheeler, morning street parking is fairly easy. But during evening peak (5 PM – 8 PM), please don't circle 12th Main like a hawk—park at the BBMP multi-level lot near 100ft Road and take the 3-minute walk under the gulmohar trees.

Pets "Can I bring my dog or cat?"

100% yes! Our shaded front verandah is completely pet-friendly. We keep clean stainless steel water bowls, and our counter staff keeps a jar of homemade dehydrated chicken-and-oat treats for four-legged regulars.

Gluten "I get bloated from regular bread. Can I eat this?"

While our kitchen mills wheat flour (so it is not certified celiac-safe), many customers who struggle with commercial supermarket bread eat our sourdough with zero discomfort. That's because our 36-hour wild lactobacillus fermentation pre-digests the complex starches and gluten proteins naturally before it ever touches the oven.

Never Miss A Loaf

Don't arrive to an empty bread basket.

Because we bake in batches of only 24 loaves per oven cycle, popular varieties like the Jalapeño Cheddar and Seeded Rye sell out fast. Send us a quick WhatsApp note and we'll label a warm loaf with your name.
