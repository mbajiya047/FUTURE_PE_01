### [STRAIGHT-TALK LOCAL FAQS]

#### 1. Remote Work: "Can I bring my laptop and work here?"
**Arjun:** "Tuesday through Friday: Absolutely yes. We have individual power outlets along all wall tables, reliable 250 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi, and our team will never hover over your table asking you to order more. On Saturdays and Sundays: We ask guests to keep the main dining tables laptop-free between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM so brunch diners and families don't face hour-long sidewalk queues."

#### 2. Parking: "Where on 12th Main can I park?"
**Arjun:** "If you ride a scooter or bike, park right in front of our verandah wooden gate. If you're driving a four-wheeler, morning street parking is fairly easy. But during evening peak (5 PM – 8 PM), please don't circle 12th Main like a hawk—park at the BBMP multi-level lot near 100ft Road and take the 3-minute walk under the gulmohar trees."

#### 3. Pets: "Can I bring my dog or cat?"
**Arjun:** "100% yes! Our shaded front verandah is completely pet-friendly. We keep clean stainless steel water bowls, and our counter staff keeps a jar of homemade dehydrated chicken-and-oat treats for four-legged regulars."

#### 4. Gluten & Digestion: "I get bloated from regular bread. Can I eat this?"
**Arjun:** "While our kitchen mills wheat flour (so it is not certified celiac-safe), many customers who struggle with commercial supermarket bread eat our sourdough with zero discomfort. That's because our 36-hour wild lactobacillus fermentation pre-digests the complex starches and gluten proteins naturally before it ever touches the oven."
