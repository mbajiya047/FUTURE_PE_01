### PILLAR 1: THE DAILY MICRO-BAKERY

#### 1. Country Classic Boule — ₹260
* **Composition:** 80% stone-ground unbleached wheat flour, 20% organic whole rye, clean water, sea salt.
* **The Crumb & Crust:** Blistered dark caramel crust with an earthy, gentle lactic acidity and an open, airy crumb.
* **Hydration:** 78%
* **Oven Schedule:** Out of the deck at 7:30 AM and 3:30 PM daily.

#### 2. Seeded Mountain Rye — ₹310
* **Composition:** 60% dark organic rye loaded with toasted sunflower seeds, golden flaxseeds, black sesame, and pumpkin seeds.
* **The Crumb & Crust:** Dense, nutty, and hearty. Exceptional shelf-life (stays fresh for 5–6 days on the kitchen counter wrapped in linen).
* **Oven Schedule:** Morning batch only (ready at 7:30 AM).

#### 3. Aged Cheddar & Pickled Jalapeño Boule — ₹380
* **Composition:** Classic sourdough folded with cubes of 12-month aged white cheddar, caramelized yellow onions, and house-pickled spicy jalapeño rings.
* **The Crumb & Crust:** Pockets of sharp melted cheese baked into the crust creating crispy cheese frico edges.
* **Oven Schedule:** Friday, Saturday & Sunday only (8:30 AM & 4:00 PM).

#### 4. Cardamom Buns & French Butter Croissants — ₹180 / ₹210
* **Composition:** 36 laminated layers of French AOP cultured butter folded into sweet wild-ferment dough, crushed Idukki green cardamom pods, and pearl sugar.
* **Texture:** Shatteringly flaky outside, soft honeycomb inside.

### PILLAR 3: THE 4 RULES WE NEVER BREAK

1. **01 · Zero Dough Improvers:**  
   No commercial enzymes, calcium propionate, or industrial yeast. If it didn't come from grain, water, and sea salt, it's not in our bread.
2. **02 · Direct Estate Sourcing:**  
   We know the Moily family who grows our beans in Chikmagalur. We pay direct fair trade premiums with zero broker middlemen.
3. **03 · Tax-Inclusive Menu:**  
   The price on the board is the exact price you pay. No unexpected 10% service charges or packaging markups when you tap your card.
4. **04 · Zero Food Waste:**  
   Yesterday's unsold loaves are sliced into garlic crostini, turned into our cinnamon bread pudding, or shared with local shelters.
