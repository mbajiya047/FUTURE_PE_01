# Modular Prompt 02: Homepage Hero, Honest Hook & Founder Story

## Instruction
You are executing the **Homepage Generation Phase** using the configuration defined in `config/business_variables.json` and adhering to the guidelines in `prompts/01_system_prompt.md`.

Generate the complete top-of-funnel website copy for **Roots & Whisk Artisan Cafe & Micro-Bakery**.

---

## Input Variables
* **Business Name:** Roots & Whisk Artisan Cafe & Micro-Bakery
* **Founder:** Arjun Sen (Tech product lead turned baker)
* **Location:** 12th Main Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru
* **Key Promise:** 36-hour wild-ferment sourdough, zero commercial yeast, direct-estate single origin coffee, quiet work-friendly weekday atmosphere.
* **Tone:** Grounded, warm, conversational, honest craftsman voice.

---

## Required Sections to Generate

### 1. Notification Banner / Top Badge
* A quick, practical operational note (e.g., morning oven schedule).

### 2. Hero Section
* **Hero Tagline / Badge:** Short, descriptive (max 6 words).
* **Main Headline:** Bold, clear, zero buzzwords. Must state what the place is and why someone should care in under 10 words.
* **Supporting Sub-headline:** 2–3 sentences elaborating on the core offering, the location context (Indiranagar), and the feeling of stepping inside.
* **Primary CTA Button:** Action-oriented, direct (e.g., *"See Today's Fresh Bakes"*).
* **Secondary CTA Button:** Practical low-friction action (e.g., *"Find Us on 12th Main"*).
* **Hero Trust Badges:** 3 concise bullet points with tangible metrics (e.g., fermentation hours, coffee sourcing, oven batch timings).

### 3. The "Honest Hook" / Problem vs. Reality
* Contrast what most commercial cafes serve (frozen pre-baked loaves, dark-roast beans burnt to mask stale flavor) with what Roots & Whisk actually does every morning.
* Keep it respectful, grounded in craft, and free of arrogant claims.

### 4. Founder's Note ("Why We Built This Place")
* 3 concise, relatable paragraphs written in the first person by Arjun Sen:
  1. *The Spark:* The frustration of working 14-hour tech shifts in Bengaluru and craving real, unadulterated bread.
  2. *The Learning:* The journey of learning sourdough fermentation from mentors in Pondicherry and understanding how flour, water, and wild yeast actually interact.
  3. *The Community:* Why this bakery is on 12th Main and what Arjun wants you to feel when you walk through the doorway.
* Sign-off: *"Arjun Sen, Head Baker & Founder"*.
