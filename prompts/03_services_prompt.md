# Modular Prompt 03: Offerings, Services & Menu Architecture

## Instruction
You are executing the **Services & Offerings Generation Phase** for **Roots & Whisk Artisan Cafe & Micro-Bakery** using `prompts/01_system_prompt.md`.

Generate an enticing, highly structured, and grounded services/offerings page. The copy must read like a passionate chef or baker explaining what they make, what goes into it, and why each item is priced the way it is.

---

## Required Pillars to Detail

### Pillar 1: The Daily Micro-Bakery (Wild-Ferment Breads & Pastries)
* **Pillar Headline & Philosophy:** Explain the 36-hour cold fermentation process and why naturally leavened bread is gentler on digestion.
* **4 Signature Loaf Cards:**
  1. *Country Classic Boule* (Flours, hydration %, crust texture, price).
  2. *Toasted Seed & Multigrain Rye* (Seeds used, dense crumb, price).
  3. *Cheddar, Caramelized Onion & Jalapeño Loaf* (Sharp cheese origin, flavor balance, price).
  4. *Morning Croissant & Cardamom Bun* (Laminated French butter pastry, batch timing, price).
* **Schedule & Pre-order note:** Clear note on when warm loaves hit the counter (7:30 AM & 3:30 PM) and how neighborhood regulars hold a loaf via WhatsApp.

### Pillar 2: The Specialty Coffee Bar (Single-Estate Direct Trade)
* **Pillar Headline & Philosophy:** Why light-to-medium roasting highlights fruit and floral notes instead of bitterness.
* **4 Coffee Offerings:**
  1. *Double Ristretto Flat White* (Tasting notes, milk texture, temperature).
  2. *Single-Origin Pour-Over (V60)* (Current estate rotation: Biligirirangana Hills washed lot, tasting notes).
  3. *24-Hour Cascara Cold Brew* (Sun-dried coffee cherry tea brewed cold, refreshing summer drink).
  4. *Iced Filter Roast with House Oat Milk* (Tasting notes, dairy-free alternative).
* **Transparent Coffee Ethics:** Explain our direct relationship with plantation growers in Chikmagalur.

### Pillar 3: The All-Day Kitchen & Tartine Bar
* **Pillar Headline & Philosophy:** Food built on top of our bread, featuring honest local farm produce.
* **3 Signature Plates:**
  1. *Whipped Ricotta & Slow-Roasted Cherry Tomato Tartine* (Olive oil, toasted country sourdough, sea salt flakes).
  2. *Smoked Chicken & Fermented Mustard Sandwich* (Pickled red onions, aged white cheddar, toasted rye).
  3. *Cast-Iron French Toast with Coorg Wild Honey* (House brioche soaked in vanilla custard, sea salt butter).

### Pillar 4: The 4 Kitchen Rules (Trust Builders)
1. *Rule 1: No Chemical Yeast or Improvers.*
2. *Rule 2: Whole Grains Stone-Ground Locally.*
3. *Rule 3: Direct Fair Pricing for Coffee Farmers.*
4. *Rule 4: Zero Food Waste (Yesterday's bread becomes crostini & bread pudding).*

---

## Voice Constraints
* No corporate phrasing (*"gastronomic paradise"*, *"epicurean delights"*).
* Provide tangible prices in Indian Rupees (₹), exact ingredients, and realistic descriptions.
