# Modular Prompt 04: Conversion CTAs, Local FAQ & Neighborhood Navigation

## Instruction
You are executing the **Conversion, Navigation & FAQ Phase** for **Roots & Whisk Artisan Cafe & Micro-Bakery** using `prompts/01_system_prompt.md`.

Generate high-converting, friction-reducing calls-to-action and honest answers to the exact questions local customers ask before visiting a cafe in Indiranagar.

---

## Required Elements to Generate

### 1. Primary Conversion Section (The "Fresh Loaf Hold" & Visit CTA)
* **Section Heading:** Clear, welcoming invitation.
* **Dual Action Modules:**
  * *Option A: Reserve Your Daily Loaf via WhatsApp* (Saves customers the disappointment of arriving at 5 PM when loaves are sold out).
  * *Option B: Come Sit With Us* (Directions to 12th Main, Indiranagar, live Google Maps link label).

### 2. Honest Local FAQs (6 Realistic Questions)
Answer with total clarity, no evasive corporate PR speak:
1. **"Is your sourdough completely yeast-free?"** (Explain commercial yeast vs. natural wild starter cultures).
2. **"Can I work from the cafe with my laptop?"** (Weekday policy with power plugs & fiber Wi-Fi vs. weekend brunch policy to keep tables open for community dining).
3. **"Where do I park my car or scooter on 12th Main?"** (Real advice on street parking vs. nearby 100ft Road designated parking lots).
4. **"Are dogs and pets allowed?"** (Verandah policy, fresh water bowls, treats).
5. **"Do you have vegan or dairy-free options?"** (Oat/almond milk coffees, olive oil sourdough loaves, vegan tartine options).
6. **"Can I order online through Swiggy or Zomato?"** (Honest explanation why fragile hot sourdough crusts are best picked up in person, but limited delivery radius is open for regulars).
